Contact us

Questions, bug reports or ideas for Sulvo: write to fahreddin@pairo.cloud. Please add your device, its system version and the game version (shown in Settings). We answer as quickly as we can.

Common questions

I bought something and it is missing

Open Settings or the Shop and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple or Google account you paid with. Remove Ads returns. Coin packs are consumed when spent, so there is nothing to restore for them.

What does Remove Ads do?

It turns off the banner on the level screen and the ads between levels for good. Rewarded ads stay available, but only if you choose them: watching one gives you something extra, such as a free hint or doubled coins.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Every level can be solved without any ad. A rewarded ad is offered only where you may trade it for a free hint, doubled level coins or a doubled daily gift.

Can I play in my own language?

Sulvo starts in your device language. To change it, open Settings › Language and pick one of the 20 languages.

Motion, vibration and sound bother me

In Settings turn on Reduce motion so that the water appears at once instead of spreading, and use the Sound, Music and Haptics switches to quiet the game. Heights are also written as numbers on the map and marked by contour lines, so colour is never the only clue.

Where do I find the player number?

It is a random code of 8 characters, found at the foot of Settings beside a Copy button. Quote it when you ask about your data or a purchase.

I have a new phone. Is my progress safe?

Progress is stored on the device only, so it does not move to a new device and is lost when you uninstall. Remove Ads can be restored with Restore purchases; stars, coins and atlas pages cannot.

My coins disappeared

Coins are spent on hints and surveys, so look there first. If a coin pack you paid for did not arrive, restart the game while online, then write to us with the store receipt and your player number.

Why is Release water greyed out?

Soil is never lost: whatever you dig up must be put down again. While the basket still holds soil the button stays off and tells you how much is left to place. Set the soil down with the bucket tool until the basket is empty.

The water failed. Did I lose something?

No. Nothing is lost when a try does not work: the water drains away, your edits stay on the map and you can change them and release the water again. A wet house is marked with a red ring so you can see what went wrong.

How are stars counted?

By the amount of soil you moved: the less you dig, the more stars. Using a hint caps the level at two stars. Undo is free and has no limit.

I am stuck on a level

Undo as often as you like. A hint lights up the next move of a best solution and costs coins or a rewarded ad. A survey shows for a few seconds where the water would go on the map as it is now. Every level can be solved.

Is the daily map the same for everyone?

Yes, the same map for every player on the same calendar day; the day comes from the date on your device. Solving it on following days builds your streak; skip a day and it starts again from one. The daily gift works the same way: a seven-day ladder that returns to the first step after a missed day.

The game crashed or looks wrong

Install the latest version and restart your device. If the problem returns, write to us with your device, system version, game version and what you were doing at the time. Crash reports reach us by themselves, but your description helps.

Delete my data

No account exists, so there is none to close. To have the data tied to your player number deleted, write to fahreddin@pairo.cloud with the subject “Delete my data” and your player number. We confirm it within 30 days. Data on your device disappears when you uninstall the game; advertising data can also be reset in your device’s ad settings.

Game version

The version is written at the bottom of Settings and on the start screen. The update page of your store shows whether a newer one exists.
